name: Trade value sections
description: Non-overlapping monetary sections in the same live Telegram dashboard.
---

Keep separate sections for public sports trades of $1,000–$5,000 (both inclusive), more than $5,000 and less than $10,000, and $10,000-and-above. Do not mix trades or totals across sections. Exactly $5,000 belongs only to the lower section; exactly $10,000 belongs only to the upper section.

**Why:** The user requested additional lower-value sections without changing the bot and website they already like. Boundaries stay non-overlapping, preserving the existing medium and upper sections.

**How to apply:** Use the selected monetary section consistently for sport/game/market lists, trade counts, graph points, captions, and filters in both Telegram and the website. The same market may appear in multiple sections, but each graph must use only its section's trades. Preserve the user's approved one-message dashboard and persist the active section through navigation and restarts. Smaller trades must not change the notification rule: fresh individual trades strictly over $10,000 only.
